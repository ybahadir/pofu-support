Support for Pofu: No Contact.

Contact us at yusufbalc88@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send journal entries, letters, or other private content unless necessary.

Subscriptions

To restore an active purchase, open Pofu and tap “Restore purchases” on the subscription screen while using the same Apple Account. To change or cancel a plan, use Apple Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancelling renewal keeps access until the current paid period ends. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple; use Apple’s purchase support for refund requests.

Private data and backups

Your journal, letters, photos, and profile are kept on your device. In You, you can export a JSON backup, import a previous backup, or delete all local data. Importing a backup replaces the current journey. Keep exported files in a place you trust.

Pofu replies

On supported devices, Pofu may reply using Apple’s on-device model. If you allow DeepSeek replies, Pofu can use a cloud service when an on-device reply is unavailable. You can turn this choice off in You → Pofu conversations. Prepared reflections remain available when a model cannot reply. See our privacy policy.

Need immediate help?

Pofu is a self-reflection tool, not emergency or professional care. If you may be in immediate danger, contact your local emergency service and a trusted person now.
