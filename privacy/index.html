Privacy policy

Pofu: No Contact is a self-reflection app. Your journal and personal space are stored on your device. Cloud AI replies are optional.

Effective October 7, 2026 · Contact: yusufbalc88@gmail.com

Information you keep on your device

Pofu stores your profile and journey dates, goals, mood check-ins, journal entries and drafts, unsent letters, photos you select, conversations, preferences, and app lock choice in the app’s local container with iOS Data Protection. The widget receives only counter and goal information through the app’s shared container. Pofu does not access your contacts or send your letters to anyone. If you export a backup, you choose where to share or store that copy.

Optional cloud replies

On eligible devices, Pofu first tries Apple’s on-device language model. If an on-device reply is unavailable, Pofu can use DeepSeek only after you choose “Allow cloud AI replies.” For a cloud reply, the app sends your current chat message (up to 800 characters), up to six recent short chat messages (up to 400 characters each), your selected reply language, and a signed Apple subscription transaction to our Google Firebase relay in the EU. The relay verifies your subscription and forwards the chat text and language to DeepSeek. DeepSeek processes this content to generate a reply. Journal entries, letters, photos, profile details, and the complete conversation are not sent with a cloud request.

You can choose “Keep chat on this device” and still receive on-device or prepared replies. Change the cloud reply choice in Pofu’s You screen at any time. Turning it off prevents future cloud requests; it cannot undo messages already processed by a provider. Please avoid including information about other people that you do not want processed by DeepSeek.

Subscriptions and service data

Apple processes App Store purchases and subscription payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information under an anonymous app identifier to help us monitor subscription status; it does not receive journal or chat text from Pofu. Our Firebase relay uses the signed Apple transaction to confirm paid access and stores hashed transaction identifiers with request counters for rate limits. Those quota records do not contain chat content and are set to expire after two days. Our application code does not intentionally log chat content or signed transactions. Service providers may process ordinary network and operational data under their own policies.

How we use and protect information

We use data on your device to provide the features you choose. We use cloud chat text only to request a reply, subscription information to confirm paid access, and limited service data to prevent abuse and operate the service. Pofu does not include ads or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat provides subscription analytics using purchase information only. Data sent to DeepSeek may be processed outside your country. We do not promise that third-party processing can be reversed after a request is sent.

Your choices and requests

In Pofu, use You → Delete all my data to remove the app’s local data, or delete the app. You may export a backup before deletion. You can also turn off cloud replies in You. To ask about information handled by our services or request assistance with a privacy concern, email yusufbalc88@gmail.com. Do not send private journal content in a support email unless necessary.

Children and changes

Pofu is not designed for children. We may update this policy when the app or its providers change; the effective date above will be updated. Material changes to optional cloud processing will be explained in the app.
